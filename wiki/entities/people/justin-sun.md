# Justin Sun

**Justin Sun** (born 1990) is the founder of [[tron|TRON]] and an advisor to and major affiliate of the **HTX** exchange (formerly Huobi), and one of crypto's most consistently market-moving individual figures — his ventures and headlines routinely move an entire cluster of correlated assets: TRX, USDD, HTX, SUN, JST, BTT, and NFT. This page covers his history factually; several episodes below (particularly the SEC litigation and the World Liberty Financial dispute) involve allegations and ongoing or recently resolved legal matters, and are presented as reported rather than adjudicated fact except where a case's resolution is itself the reported fact.

## Founding TRON

Sun founded **[[tron|TRON]]** in 2017, with the network's genesis block dated **August 28, 2017**. TRON was pitched as a high-throughput, EVM-compatible smart-contract platform, and under Sun's early leadership the TRON Foundation drove its growth into what became the dominant on-chain settlement rail for [[usdt|Tether/USDT]]. TRON's governance has since formally transitioned from the TRON Foundation to a community-led DAO structure, with Sun's role now closer to a founder-affiliate and public figurehead than a direct operator — a distinction the wiki's [[tron]] page makes explicitly ("Founded by Justin Sun (now a community-led DAO)").

## The Steemit Acquisition and the Hive Fork (2020)

In **February 2020**, the TRON Foundation under Sun's direction acquired **Steemit Inc.**, the company running the dominant front-end for the Steem blockchain and holding a large pre-mined ("ninja-mined") stake of STEEM. The acquisition — and a subsequently contested attempt to use exchange-custodied STEEM voting power to influence witness elections — triggered a community backlash that produced a **hard fork into [[hive|Hive]]**, a chain explicitly designed to exclude the Sun-controlled stake and preserve what its community considered credibly neutral governance. [[steem|Steem]]'s post-2020 governance has since been associated with Sun/TRON-aligned actors, while Hive markets itself as the community-owned continuation. This episode is a recurring reference point across the wiki for what a contested, founder-driven takeover of a DPoS chain's governance looks like in practice.

## HTX (Formerly Huobi)

Sun is closely associated with **HTX**, the exchange formerly known as Huobi, and serves as an advisor to HTX following the exchange's 2023 rebrand. **[[htx-dao|HTX DAO]]**, the governance token tied to the exchange, is native to TRON and sits within the broader "Justin Sun complex" of correlated assets alongside TRX and USDD. The precise mechanics and timeline of Sun's ownership stake in the Huobi/HTX corporate entity are not independently verified on this wiki and are not asserted here beyond his documented advisory role.

## USDD and the TRON DAO Reserve

Sun launched **[[usdd|USDD]]** in May 2022 as an algorithmic TRON-native stablecoin backed by the TRON DAO Reserve, weeks after Terra/UST's collapse; USDD briefly depegged to roughly $0.928 in June 2022 before stabilizing. It was relaunched as **USDD 2.0** in January 2025 with an overcollateralized design and a heavily subsidized ~20% APY — a yield Sun has publicly defended by stating the DAO reserve has "plenty of money" to fund it, a characterization the wiki's [[usdd]] page flags as a marketing subsidy rather than organic yield. USDD's peg integrity and collateral quality (concentrated in TRX/sTRX) are directly tied to the solvency of the broader Sun/TRON complex.

## SEC Civil Complaint (March 2023)

In **March 2023**, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filed a civil complaint against Sun, the Tron Foundation, the BitTorrent Foundation, and Rainberry Inc., alleging an unregistered securities offering and market manipulation (wash trading) involving TRX and BTT. The case was **stayed in February 2025** while the parties explored resolution under new SEC leadership, and — per aggregator (CoinMarketCap) tracking cited on the wiki's [[bittorrent]] page — was **reported dismissed with prejudice on March 6, 2026**, removing the "Alleged SEC Securities" designation that had overhung TRX, BTT, and the broader Sun-affiliated token complex. Treat the precise dismissal date as reported by aggregator coverage rather than independently confirmed here.

## World Liberty Financial Dispute (2025-2026)

Sun purchased roughly $75M of tokens in **[[world-liberty-financial|World Liberty Financial]]** (WLFI), the Trump-family-affiliated DeFi venture, during its 2024-2025 token sales. In **September 2025**, World Liberty Financial blacklisted wallets associated with Sun, freezing a large WLFI holding; Sun subsequently **sued World Liberty Financial in April 2026**, alleging extortion, wrongful freezing of his tokens, and denial of voting rights. This dispute is documented on the wiki's [[world-liberty-financial]] page as an active/recent legal matter and is presented here as reported, not adjudicated.

## Market Impact and Trading Relevance

Sun functions as a genuine **headline-risk complex** across multiple wiki-covered assets rather than a single-token story:

- **[[tron|TRX]]** — TRON's low-beta, stablecoin-demand-anchored profile is nonetheless punctuated by "Justin Sun headline risk," per the [[tron]] page's own framing, from both his ventures and past SEC action.
- **[[usdd|USDD]]** — peg and collateral quality are tied to the Sun/TRON DAO Reserve complex.
- **[[htx-dao|HTX]]** — the exchange-token side of the complex, carrying key-man and regulatory exposure tied directly to Sun.
- **[[sun-token|SUN]]**, **[[bittorrent|BTT]]**, **[[apenft|NFT]]** — smaller Sun-ecosystem tokens (governance/burn token, storage-network token, and NFT-platform token respectively) that trade as a correlated cluster on Sun-related news flow, per each page's own "Justin Sun complex" framing.
- Traders following any of these assets treat Sun-specific news (litigation outcomes, exchange developments, new venture announcements) as a distinct catalyst category layered on top of each asset's own fundamentals.
