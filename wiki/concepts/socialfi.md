# SocialFi

**SocialFi** ("social finance") is the crypto sector that puts social networks, creator economies and online reputation on-chain and attaches tradeable tokens to them: creator keys, social-graph protocols, tipping tokens and creator coins. Its promise is that users own their audience and earn from their content. In market terms it has been one of crypto's most reflexive and cycle-prone sectors: bursts of speculative activity that fade within months.

## Overview

SocialFi projects fall into a few designs:

- **Speculative creator keys** — friend.tech, launched on [[base|Base]] in August 2023, let users buy "keys" to a person's chat room, priced on a bonding curve that rose with each purchase. The creator earned a fee on every trade. Activity and fees spiked in its first months, the FRIEND token was airdropped in May 2024, and by September 2024 the developers had handed contract control to the null address, effectively abandoning the app.
- **Social-graph protocols** — portable, user-owned follows, profiles and posts that any client can read: Farcaster (with the Warpcast client), Lens Protocol (launched by the Aave team in 2022), [[cyberconnect|CyberConnect]], and the dedicated [[deso|DeSo]] chain.
- **Tipping and reward tokens** — [[degen-base|DEGEN]] began as a tipping token for Farcaster users and was one of Base's breakout tokens of early 2024.
- **Creator and content coins** — [[zora|Zora]] on Base lets posts and creators trade as their own tokens; the ZORA token launched in April 2025.
- **Fan and entertainment platforms** — projects such as [[meet48|MEET48]] and Telegram mini-app games, where engagement earns tokens.

## Why It Is Cycle-Prone

SocialFi tokens rest on a fragile loop: users join for the rewards, rewards are paid from new users' spending or token emissions, and the loop reverses once inflows slow. Retention against Web2 incumbents has been poor. The result is a familiar pattern (described on [[cyberconnect]] and [[deso]]):

1. A new primitive (keys, Frames, creator coins) goes viral on crypto Twitter.
2. Fees, users and the related tokens spike within weeks.
3. Airdrop or token-launch expectations pull in mercenary users ([[airdrop-farming]]).
4. Activity collapses after the token generation event, and tokens bleed as unlocks hit (CYBER fell roughly 98% from its September 2023 listing high).

## Trading Relevance

- **Narrative rotation.** SocialFi waves are a recurring theme in the [[narrative-trading]] playbook. They tend to arrive in risk-on phases alongside meme activity, so the [[meme-coin-cycle]] timing signals (funding, social volume, meme regime) are a useful guide to when a SocialFi move is likely to stick.
- **Airdrop and points plays.** Most SocialFi tokens launch through points programs and airdrops. [[airdrop-farming]] is usually the better risk/reward than buying after the token generation event.
- **Post-launch shorts.** Low-float SocialFi launches with heavy unlock schedules suit [[token-unlock-arbitrage]] and the post-listing fade in [[new-listing-basket]].
- **Usage as a leading indicator.** On-chain activity (keys traded, casts, tips) usually peaks before token prices do. A falling usage curve against a rising token price is a warning sign.

## Risks

- **Ponzi-like economics** — rewards funded by new entrants rather than external revenue.
- **Abandonment risk** — teams can walk away (as friend.tech's did), leaving tokens with no development.
- **Low float, high FDV** — large unlocks of team and investor supply after launch.
- **Regulatory ambiguity** — creator keys and creator coins can look like securities in some jurisdictions.
